      How the Office works

      The draw

      Your country is drawn in proportion to the number of babies born there each year. Your family's income is drawn from that country's real inequality. Nobody gets to choose, which is the point.

      The years

      Each year something happens, and every so often the Office puts a question to you about school, work, love, health or leaving. Pick a yearly focus, or switch on auto and watch.

      Your own words

      Every question accepts a written answer. An AI referee reads what you say you do, decides what is plausible for this person in this place, and files consequences that can come back years later.

      The statistics

      How long you live follows the life expectancy and child mortality of where you are. Choices bend a life; it is hard to fully escape the place you were issued.
